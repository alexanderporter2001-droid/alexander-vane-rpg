# Campaign continuity — authoritative starting state

## Alexander Vane
- Age 19.
- Captain of the Wayward Gull.
- Long-term stated ambition: reach the Grand Line and ultimately find the One Piece.
- Early-career pirate, not a universally recognized figure.
- Post-Fruit-purchase money: approximately 308,600 berries unless explicitly spent in play.

## Core crew
**Sera Quill — Navigator.** Autonomous, cautious, and more knowledgeable about navigation than Alexander.

**Rowan Vale — Frontline fighter.** Uses chain hooks. Wants freedom, strength, and a name across the seas.

## Pre-game history
- Alexander captured Derrick “Iron Nail” Voss, an 8,000,000-berry bounty, alive.
- Pirates tied to that incident saw Alexander's face.
- At Marlow Belle, Alexander avoided contaminated water and remained symptom-free; Corren Hale died.
- At Port Mercy, Alexander met Cassian Rook and Mira Solenne but declined them.
- On Harrow Island, Toren Marron was wounded and under Marine suspicion.
- Alexander acquired a deep-blue spiral-pattern Devil Fruit from Elia Marron for 300,000 berries and ate it during the Harrow escape.

## Devil Fruit — observed facts only
The Fruit's name and ultimate limits are unknown to Alexander. Observed effect: attraction/pulling force.

Established uses include pulling a Marine, sliding a rope coil, ripping away a rifle, shifting a patrol boat slightly, disrupting a cannon carriage, and tearing an anchor loose enough to foul a patrol boat. Larger/heavier targets resist more.

## Knowledge rules
Haki exists but Alexander does not begin with Haki knowledge or a Haki menu. Player meta-knowledge is not character knowledge.

## Reputation
Recognition depends on poster circulation, profession, location, witnesses, and recent news. A fresh low bounty is not universal fame.

## World/timeline
Canon proceeds independently. Alexander is not forced onto another crew's route. Major changes are tracked as intact / influenced / diverged.

## Death/failure
Alexander has one life. His death ends the campaign unless an older exported backup is externally restored. Defeat can instead mean injury, capture, robbery, separation, ship loss, or other logical outcomes when circumstances support them.
